The basics

What is competitive pricing intelligence?

Competitive pricing intelligence is competitor price, stock and price-change data for the products you sell, matched to your catalog and used to set your own prices. Its value rests on two things: correct matches and current prices.

priceleap.ai gives your pricing team both in one dashboard. The evidence behind every number is on screen.

Refresh

How often does priceleap.ai check competitor prices?

More on refresh schedules →

Each product group runs on its own schedule, with 1, 2 or 3 checks a day depending on your plan. Re-run a group on demand.

Every product shows its last fetch time, and every competitor price shows when it was last seen.

The Failed Fetches card counts products whose last check failed, so gaps in your data stay visible.
