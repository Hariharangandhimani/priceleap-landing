Why it matters

Why does product matching matter for competitor pricing?

Philips Airfryer XXL HD9650/96 PHILIPS XXL Air Fryer Blk 7.3QT Airfryer XXL (no GTIN listed)
01

The same product is listed differently on every site. Titles, abbreviations, pack sizes and colorways change, and many marketplace listings carry no shared identifier.

YOU1 unit ≠ RIVAL2-pack ↓ price cut you didn't need
02

A wrong match is worse than no match. Compare your single unit with a rival's two-pack and you cut price on a product you were never losing.

03

Manual matching breaks as the catalog grows. Thousands of SKUs across many sites, changing every week, outgrow a spreadsheet fast.
