The basics

What is real-time price monitoring?

Real-time price monitoring is the continuous collection of competitor prices, promotions and stock status from the sites where your products are sold. Instead of checking a handful of pages by hand, software captures every change as it happens, matches it to your catalogue, and shows where you stand on each SKU.
