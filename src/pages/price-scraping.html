The basics

What is price scraping software?

Price scraping software collects prices from retailer and marketplace product pages on a schedule. It turns them into structured records you can sort, compare and export.

It replaces manual checks and in-house scripts for competitor price scraping. Scraping is the collection step; price monitoring is what you do with the data afterwards.

In priceleap.ai, both live in one workspace. Collected prices go into the Price Intelligence dashboard, price alerts and pricing rules.
